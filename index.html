Scenario: Biomass Accumulation in Juvenile Malaysian Oil Palm (SFM Food Security)

The Bioscience Problem

To ensure food security, Malaysian agronomists must track the Absolute Growth Rate (AGR). By applying calculus to growth curves, we can identify the Inflection Point—the moment where the palm grows fastest before its growth begins to level off due to nutrient competition.

Function (Biomass): f(x) = L / (1 + e-k(x-x₀))
Derivative (Growth Rate): f'(x) = k · f(x) · (1 - f(x)/L)
